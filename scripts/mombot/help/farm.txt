                     
   
                  <<<< farm >>>>
   
     Visits sectors in list and farms the planets there.
     Default will visit all planets on the tl list.
            
       Usage: farm {set} {clear} {list}
            
     Options:
        {set} {sector1} {sector2} {...} {sectorx}
            Puts sectors in the order entered into the farm file.
        {clear}
            Deletes the farm file.
        {list}
            Shows all sectors in the farm file in order.
