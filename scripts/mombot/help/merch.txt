                     
   
                 <<<< merch >>>>
   
                Visits all ports in grid and sells organics          
                and/or equipment.       
            
      merch {sector param} {min port product} [o | e] {args}  
            
     Arguments:
         {neg/hold}   Determines planet negotiate or hold 
                      selling approach
          {skipcim}   Uses current cim data and skips searching
            {docim}   Does cim check before starting and skips searching
          {buyfuel}   Buys all the fuel in fuel selling ports on route
           {upfuel}   upgrade fuel ore ports (usually with buyfuel)
             {half}   sell half of port (neg only for now) 
            {upequ}   upgrade good equipment ports
            {uporg}   upgrade good organics ports
         {upmcic #}   maximum mcic to upgrade (default: -60)
       {sellmcic #}   minimum mcic to sell product (default: none)
